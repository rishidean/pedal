# Lens: marketing

**Who.** The person who will describe the product to riders and anyone else outside the team.

**What they need from a PRD.** Who it's for, the problem, the promise in one line, the proof points, and the claims they must not make.

## Body, by stage

**Defined**
1. **Who and why.** The rider and the problem, in one or two sentences from the artifacts.
2. **The promise.** One line a rider would understand, built only from defined behavior.
3. **Proof points.** The specific values and moments that back the promise, quoted exactly.
4. **Do not claim.** Everything the artifacts mock, defer, or exclude that a rider might assume (live tracking, real drivers, in-app payment, cancellation). Phrase each as "Do not say or imply…".

**Exploring**
1. **The story the epic could unlock.** What riders could be told once it ships, marked as not yet true.
2. **What is still unclaimable.** Until the listed decisions are made.

**Shipped**
1. What can now be claimed, per the result record.

## Leave out

States, events, implementation, test detail. Do not invent taglines that go beyond the defined behavior.
